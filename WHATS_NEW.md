# What's new in Dot

Plain-English notes on new features in the Dot board app, newest first, each with how to use it.
Every entry starts with a date and time (London time) so a daily check can tell what's new since yesterday.

---

## 2026-10-26 09:55 — Drag pictures straight in from Pinterest and other sites

Dragging a picture from Pinterest, Google Images or another website onto your board now adds it as a proper image card, saved in full size, instead of a link with a long code for a name.
- Drag the picture from the website onto an empty spot on the board.
- Drop it onto a gallery to add it straight to that gallery.
- Pasting a picture's address (one ending in .jpg, .png and so on) works the same way.
- Links to normal web pages still become link cards with a preview, as before.

## 2026-10-21 12:35 — Mouse wheel now zooms

If you use a mouse, the scroll wheel now zooms in and out of the board instead of moving it up and down. It zooms towards wherever your pointer is, so point at the part you want a closer look at.
- To move around with a mouse, drag an empty part of the board.
- To scroll with the wheel instead, hold **Shift** while you scroll.
- On a trackpad nothing changes: two fingers move around, pinch to zoom.

## 2026-10-20 23:30 — Welcome to your Dot board

Your board is set up and private: only your email can log in, with a code sent to you.

Things worth knowing on day one:
- **Add things** from the toolbar on the left, by dragging files or links onto the board, by pasting, or by right-clicking empty space and picking a card type.
- **Move around:** drag empty space (or one finger on a phone) to move, pinch or Ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom, **Fit** (top right) to see everything.
- **Edit text:** double-click a note or title. The bar that appears has fonts, sizes, highlights, bold, lists and more.
- **Boards inside boards:** add a Board card and click it to open it. The path at the top takes you back; drag cards onto a name in the path to move them there.
- **Galleries:** drag image cards onto a Gallery card to collect them. Drag its corner to make it wider, use − / + for photo size, and "Download all" to save them.
- **See a photo big:** select it and press Space, or double-click it (double-tap on a phone).
- **Undo** with Ctrl/⌘ + Z. Deleted things stay in **More → Recently deleted** for 30 days.
- **On your phone:** open the board in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen for an app icon.

Everything else is under **More → Keyboard shortcuts**.
